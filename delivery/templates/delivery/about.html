ABOUT FOODFIESTA

Good food, made easier.

FoodFiesta is a Django-based food delivery platform where customers can discover restaurants, explore menus, add dishes to their cart and place orders.

🍽️

Discover Restaurants

Browse restaurants and explore different cuisines available on FoodFiesta.

🛒

Easy Ordering

Explore restaurant menus, add your favourite dishes to the cart and place an order.

📦

Track Orders

Customers can view their order history and keep track of previous orders.
